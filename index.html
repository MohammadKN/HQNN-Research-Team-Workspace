Reference

Column guide

Use these definitions to keep the team’s notes consistent and comparable.

Venue / DOI
Where the paper appeared (journal, conference, arXiv) and its permanent identifier.
Baseline (parameter-matched?)
The classical model the hybrid was compared against, and whether it had a similar number of trainable parameters and equal tuning effort.
Noise Model
Ideal simulator, simulator with noise (name the source), or real hardware (device and shots).
Qubit Count
Largest circuit used in the experiments.
Code Released?
Yes or no, with the repository link.
Limitations
What the authors admit in their limitations or future work section.
Assigned to / Status
Who is reading the paper and how far along they are, so nobody duplicates work.
